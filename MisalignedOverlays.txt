Fixed on 2026-10-06: all overlays now use a PositionX that is a multiple of 8.

The Amiga code uses (x & 0xf8) for overlay positions. Each misaligned overlay got a
padded copy of its texture (overlays 133-150 in 2Overlay3D.amb and 3Overlay3D.amb)
so the result in the remake is pixel-identical and the Amiga now matches it.

tools/ambtools/validate.py reports new misaligned overlays.
