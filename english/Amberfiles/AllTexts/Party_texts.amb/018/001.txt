~LEAD~ REPORTS TO BORE ABOUT THE JOURNEY TO THE FOREST MOON AND THE FATE OF THE DWARVES. HE LISTENS INTENTLY TO THE STORIES. WHEN YOU TELL HIM ABOUT DOR KIREDON, THE CITY OF THE DWARVES ON THE GREEN JEWEL, HIS EYES LIGHT UP.^^ "SO DONNER AND THE OTHERS ARE UP THERE! BORE WANTS TO GO TO THE GREEN JEWEL TOO. TAKE BORE WITH YOU, THEN HE CAN WORK IN A MINE AGAIN!"^^ WHEN ~LEAD~ MENTIONS THAT THE MACHINES OF THE ANCIENT FACILITY ARE POWERED BY AMBER ROCKS, HE SUDDENLY TAKES NOTICE.^^ "OH, AMBER. THAT'S MY FAVORITE MATERIAL. IF YOU HAVE SOME, BORE WILL GIVE YOU THESE STRANGE STONES IN EXCHANGE. I'LL GIVE YOU THE FIRST ONE FOR FREE. JUST TELL ME THE COLOUR AND WE'LL TRADE. BORE SECRETLY BROUGHT A FEW BOXES OF THESE STRANGE STONES BACK WHEN HE WORKED FOR A FEW STRANGE HOODED PEOPLE."
