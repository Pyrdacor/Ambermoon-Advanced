ON MY EXPLORATIONS THROUGH THE MAGICAL ETHER I FOUND AN OLD TEMPLE COMPLEX WITH PORTALS TO NEW WORLDS. ONE OF THE PORTALS WAS DESTROYED, BUT I WAS ABLE TO USE IT TO ESTABLISH A CONNECTION WITH MY TOWER.^^ I ALSO EXPLORED THE WORLDS BEYOND THE OTHER TWO PORTALS. BUT WHAT AWAITED ME THERE WAS TOO DANGEROUS FOR ME. SO THAT NO ONE COULD FOLLOW ME THROUGH THE ETHER, I DREW ENERGY FROM THE PORTALS AND DIRECTED IT INTO STONES. THESE PORTAL STONES COULD STABILIZE THE CONNECTIONS THROUGH THE ETHER AGAIN.^^ UNFORTUNATELY I HAD TO LEAVE ONE OF THE STONES BEHIND IN ONE OF THE WORLDS. I HOPE THE STRANGE GUARDIANS THERE LACK THE KNOWLEDGE TO USE IT.
