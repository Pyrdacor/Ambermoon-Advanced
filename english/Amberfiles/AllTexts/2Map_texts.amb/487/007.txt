S'RIEL'S DIARY. THE LAST PAGES WERE WRITTEN ONLY RECENTLY:^ "THE COUNCIL HAS DECIDED TO SEND THE AIRSHIPS AGAINST LYRAMION. I WAS THE ONLY ONE WHO VOTED AGAINST IT. SINCE THEN S'ARIN HAS NOT LET ME OUT OF HIS SIGHT.^ SHOULD ANYTHING HAPPEN TO ME, YOU WILL FIND EVERYTHING I HAVE GATHERED ABOUT THE OTHER COUNCILLORS IN MY CHEST. MAY THE STRANGERS FROM LYRAMION BE MORE SUCCESSFUL THAN I WAS."
