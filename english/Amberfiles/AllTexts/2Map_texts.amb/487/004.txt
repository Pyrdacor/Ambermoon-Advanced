WHOEVER WANTS TO CATCH HIM MUST MEET HIM TWICE."^ S'KAT: "HIS LABORATORY REEKS OF POISON AND FEAR. THE CREATURES IN HIS CAGES ARE NOT FROM MORAG."^ S'LORWIN: "THE MIGHTIEST OF US.
