"I GREET YOU, PEOPLE OF LYRAMION. I HAVE FORESEEN YOUR ARRIVAL FOR A LONG TIME. AND I ALSO SEE THE FUTURE OF US ALL. BUT FOR NOW I WILL MAKE MY CONTRIBUTION, AS FATE HAS PREDETERMINED."^^ THE OLD MORANIAN PAUSES BRIEFLY BEFORE HE CONTINUES.^^ "HERE ON MORAG THERE WAS A VERY OLD CIVILISATION, EVEN BEFORE THE FIRST LIZARD BEINGS CAME INTO EXISTENCE. THEY WERE OUR CREATORS AND YET THEY COULD NOT PREVENT THEIR OWN DOWNFALL. THE CREATORS HAD GREAT POWER, BUT IT WAS LOST OVER THE CENTURIES. TODAY REMNANTS OF THIS POWER CAN ONLY BE FOUND IN SMALL SHARDS, SCATTERED ALL OVER MORAG. IN ADDITION, FOUR ALTARS FROM THE OLD DAYS HAVE SURVIVED. THE SAND BURIED THE REST LONG AGO. I HAVE BEEN STUDYING THESE ALTARS AND THEIR INSCRIPTIONS ALL MY LIFE AND I BELIEVE I HAVE FINALLY DECIPHERED THEM."
