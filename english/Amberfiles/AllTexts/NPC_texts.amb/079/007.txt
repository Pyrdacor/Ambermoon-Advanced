"IN THE DESERT THERE ARE FOUR ALTARS FROM ANCIENT TIMES. I WILL GIVE YOU A LIST WITH THE COORDINATES. THERE IS AN INSCRIPTION ON THE ALTARS. IT TELLS A STORY. BUT TO READ IT, YOU HAVE TO UNDERSTAND THE ANCIENT LANGUAGE. I HAVE WORKED ON DECIPHERING IT ALL MY LIFE AND FATE WANTS ME TO TEACH IT TO YOU NOW."^^ THE WISE MAN RAISES HIS ARMS AND MURMURS SOME INCANTATIONS. SUDDENLY SMALL GLOWING GRAINS OF SAND TRICKLE DOWN ON THE GROUP AND THEIR MAGIC PENETRATES YOUR MINDS. THE OLD MAN SPEAKS IN A STRANGE LANGUAGE, BUT MORE AND MORE YOU CAN UNDERSTAND THE MEANING OF THE SOUNDS. AFTER A WHILE YOU REALISE THAT YOU ARE TALKING IN A COMPLETELY NEW LANGUAGE.
